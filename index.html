About

Almost There is an alternative trio from Los Angeles, playing the city's local stages since 2025.

In October 2026 the band released its self-titled album: eleven songs, three of them featuring guest vocals from Molly Hanmer. It's out now on every major streaming platform.

Off stage, Eric Pierce and Carl Garcia work in film and television. Pierce is a University of Michigan‑trained actor and a veteran of Los Angeles theater; Garcia is an actor and cinematographer.

  • Eric Pierce Guitar, vocals
  • Carl Garcia Bass, vocals
  • Nick McKinnon Drums
